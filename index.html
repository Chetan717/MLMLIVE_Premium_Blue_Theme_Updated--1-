HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you start creating.

What is MLMLIVE?

MLMLIVE is a design platform made for network marketing professionals. It helps create marketing posters, festival greetings, rank promotions and other ready-to-share creatives.

Can I use MLMLIVE without design experience?

Yes. The app is focused on templates and simple workflows, so you can create professional-looking posts without mastering desktop design tools.

Can I use it on an iPhone?

The website links to the MLMLIVE web app at app.mlmlive.in, which you can open in a supported mobile browser. Use the Android download button for the Google Play listing.

Does MLMLIVE have background removal?

The platform includes a background-removal workflow to help prepare photos for marketing designs.

Where can I get support?

Contact MLMLIVE at admin@mlmlive.in or use the Contact page to reach the team.
